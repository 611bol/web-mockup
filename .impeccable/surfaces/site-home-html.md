# Homepage surface brief

Mode: Persuade (homepage), with the live-channel picker as an Operate-style working region. Target: `site/home.html`. Source: `ref/homepage-brief.md` (v6).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A plain, welcoming church homepage where the live picker and today's morning prayer are findable within seconds. It refuses a novelty visual world and a hero tall enough to push live below the fold.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with warm olive-tinted neutrals. The logo yellow, orange and green are accents only, never fills. Orange carries primary buttons with near-black text; dark green carries links and the live state. Noto Serif TC for headings, Noto Sans TC for body, generous line height for Chinese. Large tap targets and high contrast for elderly readers.

STORY: A newcomer sees a welcome and two buttons, 初次到訪 or 崇拜時間地點. A member sees the live picker straight away. Both leave knowing where to click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim header with logo, 5-item nav, a toolbar of search, language and staff, and the 奉獻 button. Then a single restrained hero (no carousel) with the welcome headline and the two buttons 初次到訪 and 崇拜時間地點. The top edge of the live picker shows at the bottom of the viewport.
